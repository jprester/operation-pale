Local adaptations:
surface.webp — 2048 × 1024 daytime albedo, transcoded to WebP.
earth-lights.webp — 2048 × 1024 grayscale mask of warm emissions extracted from the night composite, displayed with a warm tint on the unlit hemisphere.
earth-clouds.webp — cloud coverage converted to grayscale, resized to 1536 × 768 and transcoded to WebP.
earth.webp — a cropped still of the opening Earth rendering using all three maps, the scene lighting, and an illustrative atmosphere.

Lighting, cloud drift and accelerated rotation are artistic choices for the introduction.
