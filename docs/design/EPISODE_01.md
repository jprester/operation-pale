# Episode 01 — The Notification (proposed storyboard)

**Status:** editorial proposal. Estimated finished experience: 8–12 minutes plus optional archive exploration. Do not treat proposed voiceover as canon dialogue.

| Beat | Medium | Dramatic purpose | Production status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Earth before the signal | 3D globe, subtle ambient sound, title cards | Establish normalcy and scale | Basic placeholder implemented |
| 2. The unseen preparation | Earth–Moon diagram, Foundry stills, manifest data | Establish why the expedition is constrained | Planned |
| 3. Public Notification | Transcript with timed typography + optional narration | Explain unusual open warning | Planned |
| 4. Technical verification | Animated procedural diagrams | Show physical proof instead of a magic hack | Planned |
| 5. The opening Wave | Globe map with high-level regional sweep; no false exact precision | Establish scale and consequence | Planned |
| 6. Grafenwöhr and aftermath | Cinematic stills, eyewitness-style scene cards | Return from planetary scale to individual human experience | Planned |

## Canon checkpoints
- The notification is layered by audience and initially short; detailed technical packages follow separately.
- T+0:10 inert demonstration; T+0:15 destructive demonstration; regional Wave starts T+0:20–0:30.
- Quivers carry Scythes/Mattocks and release them after atmospheric entry.
- Do not show Monoliths already stationed over every capital at T+0. Canon says 60–80 regional units, deployed after suppression.
- The first broad occupation is **not** immediate after T+30h; a Pause and verified sites precede valuable Kestrel landings.

## Narration experiment
Try *three* modes on one beat: text-only, voiced with captions, and muted sound-design-plus-captions. Compare pacing before writing an episode's worth of voiceover.
