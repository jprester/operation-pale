# Introduction v4: script and recording sheet

**Status:** locked, with revision R1 (see *Revisions* at the end). Recorded draft in Kokoro; picture pass pending.
**Supersedes:** the narration in `src/data/audio/dialogue.json` and the narration sections of `OPENING_HOOK.md` (v3.2), `FINAL_STAGING.md` (v3.1) and `INTRODUCTION_V2*.md`. Picture and engineering notes in those files still apply where this script reuses their shots.
**Canon:** `docs/canon/CURRENT_CANON.md`, including decisions D1 (v2.4 opening, no demonstrations) and D2 (historian narrator).

## What changed and why

1. **Cold open on H-hour.** The v3 cut spent about five minutes on logistics before anything happened to people, and reached the Notification at 05:17. v4 opens with the twenty minutes before contact and the transmission itself, then goes back nineteen months to explain them.
2. **One narrator with a fixed position.** A human historian writing after the charter ended. Most of what the narrator knows came from the Company, which answered questions precisely and volunteered almost nothing. The narrator knows outcomes and is plain about the limits of the record. The narrator never knows why Earth was acquired.
3. **Canon brought up to date.** The opening follows v2.4: the Notification text is canonical, and strikes begin at the same moment. Demonstrations, the regional schedule and the 30-hour Wave are gone. Foundry Delta has its canonical role. Pre-contact staging and detection follow CURRENT_CANON §4a (D4–D6).
4. **Fewer, more specific sentences.** The generic opening ("For hundreds of thousands of years…") and the explanation of the Hegemony's motives are cut. Every line now carries a specific fact, a consequence, or a limit on knowledge.

## Voice direction

**Narrator.** Lower-middle register, dry and unhurried, about 135–145 words per minute. Read it as a lecture by someone who has read every file, not as a trailer. No upward inflection at line ends and no dramatic emphasis on numbers. Let the short sentences land flat. Pauses marked ‖ are about 0.8 s; ‖‖ is about 2 s.

**Company transmission (R1).** Voiced, but introduced as an archival recording rather than arriving as a second narrator. The narrator announces it (C0-06a). The voice is treated as a broadcast: band-limited, lightly compressed, slightly below the narrator's level, over a faint carrier that fades in a second before the first word and cuts after the last. Captions carry the speaker label "Company transmission · reconstructed". The text is canonical and verbatim; do not paraphrase it.
