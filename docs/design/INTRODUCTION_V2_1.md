# The Road to Earth

## Proposed cut and recording script

Film times run from 00:00 to 04:00. Gaps between lines hold for observation. The first six sequences use the documentary narrator. The last ends with a reconstructed excerpt from the Company.

## Geography and chronology conventions

The orthographic atlas uses a shared two-dimensional chart plane for Three.js geometry, accessible labels and the static vector edition. North is up on the plate. The overview is Sun-centred with orbital radii proportional to representative distances in AU, tilted for readability. The separate Earth-centred detail uses one mean Earth–Moon separation as its reference. Body symbols are enlarged. Planet phases, arrival track, depot locations, branch positions, cargo links and build stages are proposed illustrations. They are neither ephemerides nor facility counts. The Belt Reserve is independent insurance. Routine freight does not pass through it.

The historical record has no invented calendar dates. The operational clock starts at outer-system arrival. The edit maps elapsed film time to proposed preparation milestones of 0, 1, 4, 10, 16 and 19 months at 00:32, 01:08, 01:44, 02:35, 03:12 and 03:46. Intermediate stages illustrate cumulative growth. T-0 is public contact. The six-year charter begins at arrival and extends beyond T-0. The bundled handoff resolves Alpha, Beta and Gamma’s functions. Delta’s cargo-exchange interpretation and all four branch layouts remain provisional.
