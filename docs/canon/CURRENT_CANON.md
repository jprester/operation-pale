## 2. Premise and hard constraints

- Earth is a commercially valuable, already-emplaced industrial biosphere on an interstellar corridor. The **Hegemony** authorises a temporary venture, the **Sol Development Company**, to acquire it.
- **Charter:** six years from arrival. The Company must deliver a productive, administered Earth that needs no continuing major military commitment.
- **Casualty ceiling:** total human deaths must stay below five per cent of world population. This is a ceiling, not a target. Military personnel have no separate protected status, and below the ceiling deaths are an operating variable.
- **Technology:** about one century ahead of 2026. Interstellar travel is the only handwave. Orbital mechanics, thermodynamics, atmospheric propagation, communications delay and manufacturing limits all apply.
- **No FTL communications.** The Legate governs with delegated authority because home is months away.
- **No magic cyber.** No cryptography is broken and no keys are stolen. Control comes through physical denial, negotiated operators and authenticated channels.
- **Finite manifest.** About 3,760 t of precision hardware was shipped once and cannot be replaced inside the charter: compact fusion cores, beam directors and adaptive optics, 340,000 high-grade seekers, and dense compute. The Foundries make bulk mass and millions of crude *correlation* seekers. They cannot make one more *precision* seeker.
- **Preservation doctrine:** preserve people and expertise, the biosphere, deep emplacement (mines, dams, channels, rail beds, farmland), and the continuity of grid, water, food, medicine and nuclear safety. Anything readily rebuildable may be destroyed.
- **Human competence:** humans disperse, deceive, use weather and terrain, keep submarines, build decoys, improvise runways and preserve institutional legitimacy.

## 5. The Notification (canonical text, v2.4 §6)

> This transmission is not of human origin. Military forces worldwide are being neutralized now. This is not an attack by any human state. Civilian population centres, hospitals, food systems, water systems, and electrical infrastructure are not targets. Do not launch strategic weapons. Leave disabled military facilities and equipment immediately. Further instructions will follow.

Its purpose is nuclear deconfliction and public order. It does **not** give military crews a protected evacuation period.

**Still compatible from v2.0 §6:** separate government contact notices to command authorities, foreign ministries, space agencies, observatories and telecoms operators. A machine-readable technical identity package: root public key, message formats, Warden identifiers, challenge–response. Identity is bootstrapped by binding a signed reply to an observable physical action (Warden manoeuvre, Monolith pattern change, optical signal to a named observatory), and ordinary cryptography secures later sessions. Exact minute offsets for these are **unresolved**; do not show the v2.0 timetable.

## 6. Superseded — do not use in narration, captions or data

- The thirty-hour, night-following rolling Wave, its published regional schedule read aloud by broadcasters, and its 01:00–05:00 local-time framing.
- The v1.5 Notification text ("Get out of your vehicles … move two hundred metres away").
- The v2.0 demonstration sequence: T+0:05 bulletin, T+0:10 inert demonstration, T+0:15 destructive demonstration, T+0:18 military power denial, T+0:20–0:30 regional Wave start. **Removed by decision D1.**
- The T+30 h Pause and landing Classes 0–3. Use airfield activation stages A–F instead.
- Bounds or Monoliths over every capital at contact.
- Storks pre-positioned 6–18 h before H-hour. They now arrive after H-hour in staggered echelons.
- Wave casualties of ~340,000 and ~140,000 surviving platforms. Both were derived from the warned 30-hour Wave and need re-derivation.
- All old names in §3.
- Foundry Delta as a "cargo-exchange" facility or "role uncertain". Its canonical role is in §3.

## 8. Documentary editorial frame

- **Narrator (D2):** a human historian writing after the charter ended. Sources: Company answers to direct questions (precise when asked, rarely volunteered), recovered human tracking and observatory data, government records and testimony. The narrator knows outcomes but marks the limits of the record. The narrator **does not know why Earth was acquired**: the Company never volunteered a reason, and nobody asked in a form it recognised as a question. The film never states the reason.
- **Figure labels:** every on-screen figure is tagged *canonical figure*, *estimate* or *artistic reconstruction*.
- **Register:** observational, precise, understated. No villainy and no triumph. Administration, logistics and human perspective outweigh combat.
