## 1. Project Identity

Operation: Pale is a hard-SF alien acquisition story, not a conventional invasion fantasy. Earth is one commercial development node in a larger interstellar corridor. The Hegemony authorizes a temporary chartered venture - the Sol Development Company - to acquire a functioning planet within six years.

The tone is analytical, institutional and physically constrained. The Company is coercive and colonial, but it is not theatrical for its own sake. It values predictability, throughput, continuity and credibility. Its violence is organized around functions that must cease, not around emotional punishment.

The central aesthetic is controlled inevitability: cities remain lit while military sovereignty becomes visibly irrelevant.

## 2. Core Constraints
- **Technology:** Roughly one century beyond 2026 humanity. The interstellar drive is the sole major handwave. Orbital mechanics, thermodynamics, communications delay, atmospheric propagation and manufacturing limits still apply.
- **Communications:** No faster-than-light communication. A courier exchange with home takes months, so the Seneschal has broad delegated authority.
- **Charter:** The Sol Development Company has six years from arrival to deliver a productive, administered planet requiring no continuing major military commitment.
- **Preservation rule:** Preserve people and expertise, the biosphere, deep emplacement, and continuity of life support. Everything readily rebuildable may be destroyed when that is cheaper or safer.
- **Casualties:** The formal ceiling is five percent of humanity, but it is a ceiling rather than an objective. Current six-year estimate remains about 62 million deaths, overwhelmingly indirect.
- **Human competence:** Humans are not foolish. They disperse, exploit weather, hide under canopy, use submarines, exploit civilian terrain, fire simple weapons and create political problems the Company cannot solve from orbit.
- **No magic cyber:** The Company does not break cryptography or steal authentication keys. It controls systems through physical denial, negotiated operators, captured institutions and ordinary authenticated channels.
- **Scarcity:** The Earth-Moon Works can manufacture bulk mass, but not rapidly reproduce the precision industrial ecosystem needed for compact fusion, high-grade seekers, beam directors or dense compute.

## 3. Institutional Architecture

- **Survey Authority:** discovers and catalogs Earth. Survey personnel do not need to know the later outcome.
- **Expansion Assessment:** evaluates contact, preservation, quarantine and acquisition.
- **Sol Development Company:** temporary chartered entity with a budget, six-year deadline, casualty and ecological constraints, and rights to later production.
- **Seneschal:** composite executive authority with broad discretion because home cannot intervene in time.
- **Red teams:** independent institutions that attack the plan before launch.
- **Human governments:** retained wherever they can still operate. The Company prefers counterparties over replacement administrations.

## 4. Industrial and Logistics Architecture

The Wain is a commercial interstellar carrier repurposed for Sol acquisition. It is not a close-orbit battleship. It remains away from Earth and carries the irreplaceable manifest, command structure and seed industry.

The Works grow in cislunar space using asteroidal feedstock. Solar power is primary for industrial activity; fusion is reserved for propulsion, strategic systems and emergencies. The industrial split is fundamental:

- **Bulk difficulty:** hulls, airframes, propellant, alloy, wiring, actuators, large power systems, warhead bodies and simple optics can be manufactured locally.
- **Precision difficulty:** compact fusion cores, adaptive optics, high-grade seekers, dense compute and true semiconductor/optical ecosystems cannot be bootstrapped inside the schedule.

The expedition also establishes distributed Anchor depots and a small Belt Reserve. The reserve is not a convenient resupply base; it is insurance against a single cislunar catastrophe.

## 6. Communications Architecture

The opening message is deliberately short. It does not contain orbital ephemerides, root keys and diplomatic procedures in spoken form. Communication is layered by audience.
- **T+0:00 - Public emergency Notification:** A 45-90 second message: this transmission is not human; leave military equipment and structures; cities, hospitals, utilities and food systems are not targets; do not launch strategic weapons; more instructions follow.
- **T+0:02 - Government contact notice:** Technical contact frequencies and simple first-contact instructions are sent separately to command authorities, foreign ministries, space agencies, observatories and major telecommunications operators.
- **T+0:05 - Demonstration bulletin:** Coordinates, time, safety perimeter and description of the empty target are repeated on a distinct channel.
- **T+0:10 - Inert demonstration:** An inert Scythe penetrates the announced empty shelter. The purpose is exactness, not blast.
- **T+0:12 - Technical identity package:** Machine-readable root public key, message formats, Warden identifiers, challenge-response protocol, checksums and contact procedures become available to specialists.
- **T+0:15 - Destructive demonstration:** Mattocks demolish an evacuated hardened military complex, showing that the initial gentleness was selected rather than imposed by weakness.
- **T+0:18 - Military power denial:** Dedicated military feeds, base-local generation, shore power and similar systems are physically disabled while adjacent civilian grids remain active. This is not magical remote grid control.
- **T+0:20 to T+0:30 - Regional Wave begins:** Regional bulletins continue to repeat safety instructions and local strike windows.

Government identity is established by binding a digital key to observable control of alien platforms. A government sends an unpredictable challenge. The Company returns it signed while a Monolith changes a visible pattern, a Warden performs a tracked maneuver, or an optical signal reaches a named observatory. After that physical bootstrap, ordinary cryptography secures later sessions.

The Company also verifies governments behaviorally. A claimant proves authority by clearing a site, moving a unit, restoring staffing or maintaining a perimeter. Ceremonial legitimacy without operational control is insufficient.

## 7. Monolith Doctrine

A Monolith is a regional atmospheric service tower, not a warship or command headquarters. It combines diplomatic contact, public broadcast, navigation, weather monitoring, emergency relay and optical links. It is large and visible because visible continuity is part of its function.

Current revisions:
- Only 60-80 full Monoliths, not one over every capital.
- Smaller Beacons serve secondary regions.
- Monoliths deploy after regional air-defense suppression, not simultaneously with the Notification.
- They operate over low-density corridors outside cities, allowing damaged platforms to descend away from population.
- They use many isolated lifting cells, distributed power and distributed communications.
- They are difficult but not impossible to destroy. Some are visibly damaged; at least one is lost.
- They carry no biological leadership, unique command intelligence or irreplaceable strategic hardware.
- Destroying one is a tactical propaganda victory but strategically resembles destroying a costly alien communications tower.

## 8. Strike Logistics
- **1. Production:** The Works manufacture munition bodies, control surfaces, explosives, actuators and correlation seekers. High-grade precision seekers are assigned only when necessary.
- **2. Packaging:** Quivers are standardized disposable reentry buses carrying approximately 200 Scythes, six Mattocks, mixed loads, demonstrations or decoys.
- **3. Orbital staging:** Slow tugs distribute Quivers through several orbital planes over weeks. Wardens coordinate timing, updates, conflict avoidance and reserve assignments.
- **4. Mission upload:** Each weapon receives a primary aimpoint, expected scene, permitted impact geometry, collateral limit, alternate target and sterile abort area.
- **5. Deorbit:** The Quiver performs a small burn roughly 20-40 minutes before entry. It is committed to an atmospheric corridor rather than directly to one target.
- **6. Entry:** The Quiver's aeroshell protects many cheap munitions through the severe heating phase. This avoids giving every Scythe an orbital-grade heat shield.
- **7. Release:** At roughly 20-25 km, the aeroshell opens. Scythes unfold wings and fan across a wide footprint; Mattocks release closer to their targets.
- **8. Glide and update:** Weapons use inertial navigation, terrain or image correlation, passive optical/IR sensing and cues from Lanterns. They emit little and cannot loiter.
- **9. Terminal effect:** The weapon attacks a component or structure, not an abstract vehicle class. Abort or diversion occurs when identity, civilians or collateral conditions fail.
- **10. Assessment:** Lanterns, Harrows and local sensors classify function destroyed, probably disabled, unsuccessful, unknown or cancelled. Only urgent failures are immediately reattacked.

|---|---|---|

### Mattock effect library
| Variant | Effect | Typical use |
|---|---|---|
| Mattock-P | Tandem penetrator | Hardened shelters, blast doors, command bunkers and protected equipment rooms. |
| Mattock-K | Heavy inert penetrator | Jam portals, break runway intersections, deform structures and demonstrate capacity without explosive uncertainty. |

The canonical planning question is not "How large an explosion?" It is: "Which physical function must cease, and what is the cheapest reliable effect that produces that condition?"

## 9. Landing Doctrine

**No high-value asset descends in order to create compliance. Compliance is established first; descent is the reward.**

Kestrels are valuable reusable transports. Broad landing at T+30 is rejected. During the Pause the Company observes, negotiates, confirms essential staffing, tests sites with expendable deliveries and requires human authorities to secure the perimeter themselves.
| Class | Permission |
|---|---|
| Class 0 - Remote delivery | Expendable aid and communications only. Local authority cannot yet guarantee security. |
| Class 1 - Autonomous foothold | Disposable canisters deliver Stingers, sensors, a Herald and basic engineering equipment. |
| Class 2 - Kestrel logistics site | A Kestrel may land briefly, unload autonomous cargo and depart. No biological staff or unique command hardware. |
| Class 3 - Strategic gateway | Repeatedly stable site with redundant authority, secured corridors and verified infrastructure access. Reeves and high-value components may descend. |

First landings are freight deliveries, not ceremonies. Hands and Forges arrive before Reeves. Biological Company personnel and unique manifest hardware are withheld until a site has demonstrated long-duration control.

## 10. Naming Doctrine

Administrative and institutional terms may retain the translated medieval vocabulary: Wain, Works, Seneschal, Reeve, Tithing. Hardware names should be short, concrete and visually distinct. The revised operational chain is:

**Warden -> Quiver -> Scythe or Mattock -> Lantern confirmation**

The surface chain is:

**Kestrel -> Stingers secure -> Hands operate -> Forges rebuild -> Herald communicates -> Reeve negotiates**

## 12. Firm Later-Campaign Canon

- The precision-seeker magazine is exhausted in Month 14.
- Human air forces discover a genuine weather gap and preserve it for years; the Company never fully closes it.
- The Domesday is a mapped sensor layer, not a blanket. Resistance eventually publishes its edges.
- Only four of fourteen strategic submarines are tagged before T-0. The hunt lasts months, and one boat is never found until its crew surfaces from logistical necessity.
- The satellite ground segment takes about five months to rebuild, not weeks.
- Commercial satellite operators retain their own staff, credentials and keys. The Company compels service through physical and political control, not cryptographic theft.
- Organized resistance above battalion scale ends in Month 19.
- Charter conditions are met in Month 71, one month inside schedule.
- Six-year casualties remain about 62 million, or 0.78 percent of humanity. Most deaths arise from logistics, state collapse and disrupted trade rather than direct alien weapons.
- The grid remaining active is the largest reason casualties do not rise by an order of magnitude.
